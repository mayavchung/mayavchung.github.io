## 🌍 Current Research Areas

Here are a few highlights of my current work. For full publications, see the [Publications](/publications/) tab.

---

### 🔬 Climate Variability and Disease Spread
![ENSO and health](/images/project1.jpg){: width="250" style="float:right; margin-left:20px;"}
I investigate how large-scale climate patterns like El Niño–Southern Oscillation (ENSO) influence the spread of infectious diseases over seasonal to multiyear timescales.  
[Read more →](/publication/2025-paper-enso-disease)

---

### 🌊 Ocean Salinity and Nudging Experiments
![Salinity nudging](/images/project2.jpg){: width="250" style="float:right; margin-left:20px;"}
Using climate models and ocean experiments, I study the role of surface salinity in shaping tropical Pacific circulation and variability under fixed-boundary conditions.

---

### 📊 Climate Modeling for Policy Impact
I work on connecting large-scale climate model outputs to public health and adaptation decisions, focusing on actionable uncertainty and multiyear predictability.
